# Commercial launch plan — Product Ad Factory

## First customer segment
UAE small e-commerce brands and Shopify store owners with 10–300 SKUs; home, gifts, accessories and consumer electronics. Avoid regulated and high-claim categories initially. Position as reliable managed ads, not an unproven all-purpose AI video tool.

## Pricing (proposed, not verified market averages)

| Offer | Price | Scope |
|---|---:|---|
| Single Ad | AED 249 | 20s vertical MP4, one approved product, one revision, original soundtrack |
| Growth (assisted) | AED 1,499/month | Eight 9:16 ads/month, consistent branding, concierge management |
| Scale (assisted) | AED 3,999/month | 24 9:16 ads/month, two revisions and priority service |
| Arabic or English adaptation | +AED 99/video | Separate reviewed-language render, manually arranged |

No recurring charges are initiated by this MVP. Prices exclude VAT if applicable. Standardize revisions (copy and timing, not replacing entire product or creative campaign).

## Illustrative economics
GitHub published Linux 2-core price: $0.006/minute. At eight runner minutes per render this is $0.048 (~AED 0.18 at about 3.67 AED/USD), excluding included quotas. Actual video costs require measuring full CI job duration, storage, editing, claim verification, revisions and acquisition expenses. Remotion license eligibility depends on legal entity and its workforce; Automators is listed at $0.01/render with $100/month minimum for ineligible organizations. Check official terms before selling.

Sample revenue:
- 3 Single Ads: AED 747 one-time gross
- 5 Growth clients: AED 7,495 monthly gross revenue
- 10 Growth clients: AED 14,990 monthly gross revenue
These are scenarios, NOT forecasts or profit estimates.

## First 30 days
1. Rights-cleared portfolio: 3 videos in separate product categories, one English and one Arabic variation.
2. Prospect 25 owner-operated online stores. Personalize outreach from their actual product category, not generic mass spam.
3. Have 15 customer conversations, send 10 appropriate video samples/proposals, and aim for 3 paid pilot orders.
4. Track minutes of manual claim review, asset cleanup, revisions, quote-to-payment conversion and turnaround.
5. Ask customers whether they would pay AED 1,499 for eight videos/month; do not build recurring billing until evidence supports it.

Days 31–60: reusable brand kits, better aspect ratio support, richer audio and 2 recurring managed clients.
Days 61–90: credit ledger, billing integration, tenant storage, private workflows and conditional SaaS beta after ~10 repeat customers.

## Legal and commercial requirements
- Establish an appropriate UAE commercial activity and verify local media/advertising permit obligations.
- Have client image, trademark and advertising claim permissions documented; marketplace listing access does not imply reuse rights.
- Define invoice terms, project scope, cancellation, refunds, delivery, retention and media rights in a written customer agreement before receiving payments.
- Keep confidential media and personal information private; use a private repo for live rendering, with environment protection and spending limits.
